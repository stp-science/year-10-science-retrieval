Year 10 Science · Activity 02

Match each volcano
to its type.

Inspect each photograph and choose its volcano type. A correct match reveals the reason; an incorrect match gives you a hint. Select any photo to enlarge it.

Look beyond the name. Use the profile, slopes and size of any depression. Open the evidence clue if needed. A crater alone does not prove a caldera, and a photograph may hide part of the volcano.
