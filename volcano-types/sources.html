Learning sources

The activities use slides 5–10 of the supplied class presentation, with Year 10 summaries and checks against the following sources.

  • Science Learning Hub — Types of volcanoes: the link in slide 5 and the main reading source.
  • Te Ara — Volcano types: composite cones, volcanic fields and calderas.
  • GNS Science volcano fact sheets: Tarawera is a dome volcano within a caldera.
  • Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program — Barren Island: cone within a caldera.
  • USGS — Mauna Loa: shield volcano.
  • USGS — Principal types of volcanoes: volcano structures and formation.

Notes for the teacher

The combined “scoria field / shield” category follows the slides. The comparison page explicitly separates a volcanic field (an area) from a scoria cone and a shield (landforms). Crater Hill is a maar within the Auckland field.

Tarawera uses “more complex / another type” because it is a lava-dome complex within Ōkataina caldera. Barren Island accepts either cone or caldera, with an explanation of the scale being classified. Written reasons are compared with feedback, rather than automatically awarded marks.

The photograph labelled “Vakak (Afghanistan)” in slide 10 has been replaced with Mauna Loa, a well-documented shield example. That original image could not be reliably identified as a volcano. Tafua Upolu is retained using the name in the slides.
