Notes for the teacher

The definition cards distinguish composite cones, scoria cones, shield volcanoes and calderas. Students summarise all four and write three comparisons. A volcanic field is explained as an area containing volcanoes, rather than a volcano shape.

Matching uses these four categories. Crater Hill (a maar) and Tarawera (lava domes within a caldera) are omitted from this four-type matching task because they do not fit the categories cleanly. Rangitoto is classified by its main shield, with its summit scoria cones explained in feedback. Barren Island accepts composite cone or caldera because a younger cone sits within an older caldera.

The photograph labelled “Vakak (Afghanistan)” in slide 10 has been replaced with Mauna Loa, a well-documented shield example. That original image could not be reliably identified as a volcano. Tafua Upolu is retained using the name in the slides.
