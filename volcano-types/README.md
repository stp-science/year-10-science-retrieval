# Volcano Lab — Year 10
Three separate activity pages based on slides 5–10 of Volcano Types:
- `index.html`: compare and describe volcano types, with research summaries and writing spaces.
- `sort.html`: 16 photograph cards, explanations, evidence clues, image enlargement and feedback.
- `label.html`: keyboard- and touch-accessible labelling of a composite volcano.

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript, with no build step. Serve this directory over HTTP. Responses save in localStorage on the current browser; nothing is sent to a teacher or server. Print supports PDF through the browser.

Research and image credits, scientific corrections and example substitutions are documented in `sources.html`.
